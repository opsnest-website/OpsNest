FocusGuard: screen-time limits that hold.

Our first app. It locks an app when the day's time is used up, and lets someone you trust know if you break a limit.

Set a daily limit for any app, share one budget across a group of apps, or block apps at set times such as bedtime. Turning protection off takes your password, and limits can't be loosened on impulse.

No account, no ads and no tracking. Everything FocusGuard records stays on your phone, encrypted.

FocusGuard comes in two versions: the full version from our website, with the strictest protection, and a Google Play version (coming soon) that follows Play's rules.
